# 🔔 Pick your roles
Use Snitch's menu in this channel to choose what you want. Picking again replaces your choice, and clearing everything stops the pings.

🛠️ **Build stream pings**: a ping when a build stream starts (CAD, printing, homelab, code)
🎮 **Gameplay stream pings**: a ping when a gameplay stream starts
🕹️ **Co-op gaming**: get called up for co-op sessions
🧱 **3D printing and makers** · 🖧 **Homelab and code**: show what you're into
🎨 **Digital art**: opens the Digital Art rooms
✒️ **Creative writing**: opens the Creative Writing rooms
🏆 **Esports League**: join the Magenta Forge League (pick Co-op gaming too)
