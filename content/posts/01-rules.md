# 📜 The Magenta Forge Rules
Welcome to the Forge. These rules are not suggestions. Breaking them gets a warning, a timeout or a ban, depending on how bad it is, and staff decide which.

**1. Respect people as they are.**
Use the name and pronouns people give you. Misgendering on purpose, deadnaming, or "just asking questions" about anyone's identity is a ban, not a warning.

**2. Zero tolerance for hate.**
No racism, transphobia, homophobia, sexism, casteism, ableism or slurs, including "jokes", reaction images and usernames.

**3. No harassment, no doxxing.**
Don't post anyone's real name, address, school, workplace or photos without their consent. This includes Amaya. Don't follow people into DMs after they've said no.

**4. Keep it SFW.**
No NSFW, gore or shock content anywhere, including profile pictures and statuses.

**5. No spam or self-promo.**
No ads, unsolicited invites, referral links or DM advertising. Sharing your own build in #maker-workbench is welcome; dropping links and leaving is not.

**6. Spoilers go under spoiler tags.**
Story spoilers for Cyberpunk 2077, GTA 6 and anything recent go inside `||spoiler tags||`.

**7. No cheats, piracy or mod menus.**
Don't share cracked software, cheat tools or GTA Online mod menus. Single-player mods for Cyberpunk are fine.

**8. Voice rooms are public.**
Anyone in a voice room can hear you, and voice can be streamed. Don't say anything there you wouldn't say on stream.

**9. Be 13 or older.**
Discord's Terms of Service apply here too. Accounts under 13 are removed.

**10. Staff have the final word.**
If a mod asks you to drop a topic, drop it. Disagree with a decision? Open a private ticket in #open-a-ticket instead of arguing in public channels.
