# ✅ Verification help
Stuck getting in, or your role didn't show up? Use the **Snitch buttons in this channel**. This channel is read-only, so nothing you send gets lost in a busy feed, and **never post your email, payment receipts or personal details anywhere public**.

**Free access: become a {{TOURIST_NAME}}**
1. Press **Send my intro**. Snitch DMs you the intro template.
2. Fill it in and send it back to Snitch.
3. A mod reviews it. Once approved, you get the {{TOURIST_NAME}} role.
Your intro is only seen by staff. You must be 13 or older.

**Supporter access: link Buy Me a Coffee**
1. Join a membership at {{BMC_URL}}.
2. Press **Link my membership** and follow the two steps: sign in with Discord, then enter the code sent to your Buy Me a Coffee email.
3. Your supporter role arrives within a few minutes.
Use the same email you used on Buy Me a Coffee.

**Still stuck?**
Press **Ask for help** and a mod will reply privately. Already a member? Open a ticket in #open-a-ticket.

**Common fixes**
• *"I paid but have no role."* Check you used the same email, then press **Link my membership** again.
• *"My membership ended."* You keep your role for a 7-day grace period, so nothing disappears overnight.
• *"Snitch won't DM me."* Turn on *Allow direct messages from server members* in this server's privacy settings, then press the button again.
