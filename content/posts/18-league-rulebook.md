# 🏆 Magenta Forge League rulebook
Four teams, friendly rivalry, real standings: **Hammer**, **Tongs**, **Bellows** and **Rust**. Pick **Esports League** (with **Co-op gaming**) in #pick-your-roles to sign up, and a commissioner places you on a team.

**Games**
GTA Online, GTA 6 and Fortnite, with Minecraft and Cyberpunk 2077 as side quests.

**Scoring**
• Head-to-head: win **3**, draw **2**, loss **1**
• Free-for-all: 1st **4**, 2nd **3**, 3rd **2**, 4th **1**
• **+1** for fielding a squad on time
• **+1 Best Sport** to the kindest team of the night
• A forfeit scores **0** and the other side wins
• A tie on points and Best Sport is settled with rock-paper-scissors in **The Arena**

**Rules**
1. Fixtures are posted in #fixtures, in India time. Snitch reminds you a day, an hour and 15 minutes before.
2. Captains confirm squads in #league-office before the start.
3. A match room opens 15 minutes before kick-off for the teams playing.
4. Results go in #scoreboard; write-ups and clips go in #match-reports.
5. No cheats, mod menus or exploits. That's a forfeit, and a ban from the league.
6. Conduct: trash talk stays playful. Abuse, slurs or rage-quitting to ruin a match voids your team's points for that night.

The commissioners' calls are final. Questions? Ask in #league-office.
