# 🎨 Art Studio
For digital illustration and anything visual: sketches, finished pieces, works in progress, palettes, brushes and process videos.

• Share your own work, and credit others when you share theirs.
• Ask for critique if you want it; otherwise, assume people want encouragement.
• No AI-generated art passed off as hand-made. Label it if you post it.
• Keep it SFW, as everywhere else.

Want company while you draw? Join the **Art Studio** voice room.
