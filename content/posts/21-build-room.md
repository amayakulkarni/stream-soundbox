# 🔧 Build Room
A focus room for working on your own projects alongside others.

Start a session with `/pomodoro start`: focus blocks and breaks take turns, the room plays background ambience during focus, and Snitch repeats the room's to-do list from `/todo`. Press **Mute me during focus** if you want to be muted until the break. `/pomodoro stop` ends it.
